# EarthTrend — Audit, Data Validation and Scientific Review (2026-11-15)

Branch: `claude/earthtrend-audit-validation-ed1356`. Every number below comes
from commands run during the audit (listed in section H). Nothing was assumed
from documentation alone.

The statistical decision was pressure-tested with an "LLM council": five
independent reviewers (contrarian, first-principles, expansionist, outsider,
executor) plus five anonymous peer reviews. They agreed on fixing the SST data
first, removing blanket withholding, drawing a deseasonalized trend line and
connecting the unused datasets. They disagreed on Newey-West (HAC) errors. A
simulation settled it: HAC gave 25–54% false positives and was rejected (see G).

## A. Overall assessment

- **Alignment with the challenge.** Yes, after the fixes. Users choose a
  variable, a location (POWER point) or region (Giovanni area average), a
  period and a resolution. The app plots real NASA data, estimates direction
  and rate, and tests significance with plain-language interpretation.
- **Scientific reliability.** Good for a hackathon tool, with clear limits (G).
  The statistics were already careful. They were so careful that they answered
  "is it significant?" for almost none of the Giovanni data.
- **Ready to present?** Yes for the data and statistics. See section I for the
  remaining items: attribution wording, data-use policy and a rehearsed demo path.
- **Biggest weaknesses before the audit.** (1) The Aqaba SST file was shifted
  by one month for 2005–2021. (2) Four of the five downloaded Giovanni datasets
  were not reachable from the app. (3) Significance was withheld for every
  21-year Giovanni series at annual resolution and for most at monthly. (4) No
  trend line on monthly charts.

## D. Remaining issues (not fixed, and why)

- **Data-use policy and citation wording** for GES DISC, OB.DAAC and NASA POWER
  are still unverified (`DATA_SOURCES.md`). This needs a person to read the
  policies; it was not guessed.
- **SST unit** is not stated in the Giovanni download; Celsius is inferred from
  the values and labelled as inferred.
- **Raw Giovanni exports are not in git.** Only EVAP and SST raw files were
  found locally (`Downloads`); EVAP matched the processed file exactly. The raw
  T2M, PRECTOTCORR and GWETTOP exports were not found, so those processed files
  could only be checked for internal consistency (252 months, no gaps or
  duplicates, plausible ranges and seasonal cycle). MERRA-2 Giovanni stamps are
  first-of-month at 00:00 (verified on EVAP), so the SST stamp problem does not
  apply to them.
- **No multiple-testing correction.** Testing many variables/periods raises the
  chance of a false "significant" somewhere; stated in `docs/ANALYSIS.md`.
- **Some results stay withheld by design**: annual EVAP and annual SST
  (residual dependence beyond lag 1 at n = 20–21). The app says why.
- **No free-form regions.** POWER is point-only; Giovanni regions are the two
  that were downloaded. Adding regions needs new Giovanni exports.
- **No offline fallback for POWER.** If the API is down at demo time, use the
  Giovanni source.

## G. Scientific reliability

- **Decision rule (final review):** OLS trend slope; significant only if both
  the AR(1) effective-sample-size test and the fixed-b test (Kiefer-Vogelsang,
  no autocorrelation model) reject; p = the larger p-value, interval = union of
  both intervals, so interval and verdict always agree. Monthly data are tested
  on annual means of deseasonalized values, so monthly and annual give the same
  answer. Dependence beyond lag 1 still withholds. 15–29 years: "indicative".
- **Secondary:** Mann-Kendall (lag-1 variance correction), not part of the
  decision. Sen slope is descriptive (seasonal Sen for monthly).
- **False-positive rates (SYNTHETIC, out-of-sample seed, nominal 5%), n = 21:**
  independent 2.3%, AR(1) 0.5 4.7%, AR(1) 0.8 7.0%, lag-2 10.5%, AR(2) 16.2%
  (previous rule: 4.0 / 6.7 / 9.5 / 17 / 22%). n = 35: 1.0–4.5% for AR(1) up to
  0.8, AR(2) 9.5%. Monthly 21 years: 1.7–4.7%. Power at 0.08 SD/yr: n = 21 28%
  (was 40%), n = 35 81% (was 86%). Full tables and rejected alternatives (AR(p),
  sieve bootstrap, REML, HAC) in `docs/ANALYSIS.md`; checks in
  `tests/test_false_positive_rates.py`.
- **Known limits:** strong multi-year persistence at 15–29 years still gives
  10–17% false positives; a step change (e.g. sensor change) is reported as a
  trend 13–37% of the time; no multiple-testing correction.
- **Distinctions the app makes:** increasing / decreasing (sign of the
  estimate), statistically significant or not, "Not tested (see notes)", and
  insufficient data (< 3 values, or < 3 years for monthly seasonal separation).
- **Limits:** 21 years is short relative to decadal variability; the start or
  end year can change a result; reanalysis trends can include changes in the
  observing system; no causation is claimed (warning shown on every result).
